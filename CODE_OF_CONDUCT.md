# Code of Conduct

This project expects professional, evidence-based collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat clinical-safety concerns as first-class engineering issues.
- Use synthetic or de-identified examples when discussing behavior.
- Be direct, specific, and respectful in technical disagreement.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on reproducible facts, tradeoffs, and fixes.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Sharing real PHI, credentials, private audit logs, or patient-identifying screenshots.
- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Pressuring maintainers to weaken safety gates without evidence and review.
- Misrepresenting Aegis as FDA-cleared, HIPAA-certified, or production-ready.

Maintainers may remove content, close issues, or restrict participation when this policy is violated.
